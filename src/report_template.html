Overview

RNA-seq data from three ocular tissues (cornea, limbus, and sclera) were analyzed independently using a publicly available SARS-CoV-2 infection dataset (GSE164073). Principal component analysis (PCA) and sample-distance heatmaps were used for quality control to assess sample clustering patterns. Differential expression (DE) analysis was performed to identify differentially expressed genes, followed by Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA) to characterize enriched biological pathways and functional annotations.

The reanalysis reproduced several key findings from the original study. Chemokine genes CXCL1, CXCL3, and CXCL6, associated with inflammatory activation and immune cell recruitment, showed increased expression following SARS-CoV-2 exposure. In addition, TNFAIP3, SOD2, C3, and RIPOR3 were consistently upregulated across at least two ocular tissues, indicating shared host-response signatures.

Among the three tissues analyzed, the limbus exhibited the largest number of significantly downregulated differentially expressed genes. GSEA further identified downregulated processes associated with epithelial structure and differentiation, including epithelial cell development, epidermis development, and intermediate filament cytoskeleton organization, as well as pathways involved in cornified envelope formation, tight junctions, adherens junctions, cadherin signaling, and cell adhesion molecule interactions. Overall, the reanalysis supports the reproducibility of SARS-CoV-2-associated transcriptional changes and functional pathways previously reported.
